# AURUM Master Vision

AURUM is an Institutional Market Operating System designed to run a complete daily investment workflow.

It integrates market data, research-council explanations, memory, decision intelligence, governance, portfolio directives, execution readiness, reliability monitoring, and institutional reporting. Research explanations do not choose weights or issue trades.

## Mission

Turn AURUM into a reliable, auditable, scalable, deployable, and maintainable platform that can run every day.

## Phase 6A Objective

Phase 6A converts AURUM from a collection of advanced research modules into an institutional engineering system.

## Core Principles

- One operating command
- One official dashboard
- Provider-independent market data
- Durable database persistence
- Self-monitoring reliability
- Containerized deployment
- Auditable outputs
- Human-governed execution

## Completion State

At the completion of Phase 6A, AURUM becomes an Institutional Market Operating System.
